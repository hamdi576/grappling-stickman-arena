## 🎯 About The Game

**Grappling Stickman Arena** combines momentum-driven grappling hook physics with fast-paced stickman combat. Swing across floating platforms, build up velocity to unleash high-speed energy smashes, hurl spears in mid-air, and earn up to 3 stars on every stage.

### ✨ Key Features
* **True Pendulum & Swing Physics:** Latch onto glowing aerial anchor points and use momentum to launch yourself across the arena.
* **High-Speed Smash Attacks:** Reach critical velocity to activate a kinetic aura that knocks enemies out of the ring on impact.
* **Mid-Air Spear Throwing:** Snipe distant enemies while swinging through the air.
* **3-Star Stage Progression:** Complete stages with high health and smash knockouts to unlock all 3 stars per level.
* **Upgrade System & Auto-Save:** Spend earned coins to upgrade Hook Range, Swing Boost, Spear Damage, and Max Health—automatically saved in your browser via `localStorage`.
* **100% Self-Contained HTML:** No frameworks, no build tools, and no external assets required.
